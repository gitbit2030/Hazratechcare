Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how HazraTech Care handles information when you visit this website, contact us, request home computer service, or use product recommendation links.

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only when needed to respond to your request, provide service, comply with legal obligations, prevent misuse, or work with service providers that help operate the website or communication channels.

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you provided to us, subject to service, recordkeeping, and legal needs.

For privacy questions, contact HazraTech Care through the details on our Contact page.

Policy Updates
